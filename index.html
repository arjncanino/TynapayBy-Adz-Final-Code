History & Establishment

April 2020: Adrienne Ty (“Adz”) and Eric Ty established the business during the COVID-19 lockdowns. Baking began as a therapeutic outlet and an alternative source of income from their home kitchen along the borders of Cainta and Pasig.

The name is a wordplay on tinapay, while “Ty” honors the family surname and “T.Y.” expresses gratitude to God and their supporting community.

The business began with an order of one dozen Ube Cheese Pandesals. Its menu grew to include Classic Cheese Rolls, Cinnamon Rolls with Cream Cheese Glaze, and Coffee Buns. From 2024 onward, the TYnapay + Kape Mobile Event Cart expanded its service to corporate events, brand activations, and weddings.

Founders: Adrienne Ty (“Adz”) and Eric Ty.
